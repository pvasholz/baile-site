What the dimensions mean
Limitations of this display

Each cell is a single run. There is one response per model per text per condition, scored once. Differences of a point between two models are not evidence that one model is better than the other; they are evidence about these two responses.

The grid is ragged, and shown as such. Only the Ariel condition was scored against the rubric, so unconstrained panels display response text with no score bars. Several unconstrained runs were never collected — those cells say so rather than silently substituting something else.

Scores are on a 0–2 scale per dimension. They are one rater's application of a written rubric, not a consensus judgment, and no inter-rater agreement is reported for this corpus.

What your rating collects

Scoring a response yourself is entirely optional, and the rubric scores are shown whether or not you do. Nothing here is gated behind a rating.

If you do give a score, we store the number you chose, which response it referred to, and a random session identifier generated in your browser. No account, no IP address, no free-text field, nothing that identifies you. The identifier exists only so several ratings within one visit can be grouped, and it is discarded when you close the tab.

Ratings may inform later work on the rubric. No thanks dismisses the prompt and nothing is sent.
