Research

Three programs, one institute.

These are starting points, not finished departments. The first task is to make the questions crisp enough to deserve projects.

01

Evaluation

Agenda in formation

We build measures of how well language models maintain epistemic restraint: whether a model stops where its grounds stop, gives a reason when a question resists settlement, and answers what was asked instead of an easier substitute. Project Ariel is the first instrument in this program.

  • · Epistemic restraint rubrics
  • · Grounded vs. named uncertainty
  • · Evasive substitution
  • · Cross-model comparison

02

Memory

Agenda in formation

We are working towards a memory system built on an enactivist account of memory and cognition — retrieval as reconstruction under present demands, rather than lookup against a fixed store — and towards ways of measuring whether such a system behaves differently from a retrieval index.

  • · Enactivist memory models
  • · Retrieval state and recall
  • · Reconstruction over lookup
  • · Scoring memory-conditioned runs
