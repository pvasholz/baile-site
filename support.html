How to help

Baile is small, so most kinds of help make a visible difference. These are the ones that matter most right now.

Evaluation

Become a rater

Ariel's scores come from one human rater and two AI raters. More people applying the full rubric would show how much the scores depend on who is doing the scoring. If you'd be willing to score a batch of responses, get in touch.

Rate a few responses

Scoring a response yourself in the comparison tool adds an outside view to set against the rubric scores. It's anonymous, and there's no rubric to learn.
